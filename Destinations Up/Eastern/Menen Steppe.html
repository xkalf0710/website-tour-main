Menen Steppe

Location

The Menen Steppe, located in the eastern part of Mongolia near the border with China, is one of the largest and most expansive grassland areas in the country. Stretching for hundreds of kilometers, this vast steppe is characterized by its open, flat terrain, covered in rich grasses that have sustained nomadic herders and their livestock for centuries. The Menen Steppe is part of the Eurasian Steppe, making it an essential part of Mongolia’s traditional nomadic lifestyle and agricultural economy. The steppe is home to a variety of wildlife, including Mongolian gazelles, which migrate across the region in large herds. Birdwatchers also frequent the area, as it is a habitat for various species of birds, especially during migration seasons. Its boundless, open landscape offers visitors a true sense of Mongolia’s vastness and natural beauty...

...For travelers, the Menen Steppe is a peaceful and remote destination that provides a unique opportunity to experience the wide, unbroken horizon of Mongolia’s iconic steppe environment.
