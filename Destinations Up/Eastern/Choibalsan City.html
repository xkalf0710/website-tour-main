Choibalsan City

Location

Choibalsan City is the capital of Dornod Province, located in the easternmost part of Mongolia. Named after the Mongolian communist leader Khorloogiin Choibalsan, the city plays a crucial role as an economic and administrative center for the eastern region. Choibalsan lies along the banks of the Kherlen River, which flows from the Khentii Mountains through the eastern steppe. Although it is relatively remote and less visited compared to other cities in Mongolia, Choibalsan has historical significance and serves as a gateway to the eastern steppe, including important natural reserves and wildlife habitats. The region is home to diverse wildlife, including Mongolian gazelles, and is a key area for Mongolia’s agriculture and livestock industries. Visitors to Choibalsan can explore local attractions, such as the Choibalsan History Museum, which provides insights into the city’s past and the region's role in Mongolia’s history...
